How it works

  1. 1

    You choose the sources

    Slack, Linear, GitHub or GitLab, Notion, Google Drive, Calendar and more. Nothing you do not tick is read.

  2. 2

    Chef investigates your week

    Only your own activity, read-only. It checks threads, tickets, docs and meetings for what you built, decided, taught and unblocked.

  3. 3

    You get a private report

    A short roast with a verdict, then the receipts for every claim. Sharing is optional, and you review exactly what leaves.

Chef can also ask, "Anything I can't see?" Unread counts and saved items are invisible to the tools, so you can tell Chef about them. Open loops, such as tags with no visible reply, are off unless you switch them on.

Fictional example

The verdict

A good week, and a busy one, which is often the same thing. You got three teams to agree an order for a migration nobody wanted to start, and eleven small fixes are waiting for someone to look at them. Both facts are true, and only one of them is your fault.

Starter

Monday opened with a billing migration that two teams each believed belonged to the other. By Wednesday it belonged to a plan with your name on it, which is how these things usually go.

Nobody volunteered. You wrote the thing down, which in most organisations counts as volunteering.

Main

The plan itself was simple. Small accounts first, large ones later, finance told the dates before anyone else could be surprised by them. The platform lead built a rollout calendar from it within a day, which is the closest an engineer comes to a standing ovation.

The part worth mentioning is that three teams agreed to an order. That usually takes a meeting, then another meeting about the first meeting. You did it in one thread.

Every joke traces to something observed. If the evidence does not support a joke, the joke is cut. The facts are sacred. The commentary is not.

Pick your sources

Connect the tools you already use in Claude, then tell Chef which to read. Open a source to see exactly what it reads and what it never reads. You can also pass sources=slack,linear or skip=calendar.

Chat

Slack

Reads Your own messages, day by day, plus the threads they sit in, reactions on your posts and the links you shared. Used to understand what you decided, taught, thanked people for and shipped, not to count messages.

Never reads Unrelated conversations, sensitive one-to-one threads beyond their category, huddle audio, file contents, or anything not connected to your own messages.

Microsoft Teams

Reads Only messages you wrote, searched for without surrounding context: counts, timing and themes.

Never reads Surrounding messages, other people's replies or threads, meeting recordings or transcripts of calls.

Tasks & projects

Linear

Reads Issues assigned to you or created by you that changed, projects you lead or belong to, items stuck in review.

Never reads Other people's issues, private teams you aren't part of, or comments by others beyond counts.

Jira

Reads Tickets assigned to you, reported by you or transitioned by you, and what stayed stuck.

Never reads Other people's tickets, boards you don't use, or private comments from others.

Asana

Reads Tasks assigned to you or completed by you, projects you own, overdue and parked items.

Never reads Other people's tasks and private projects you don't belong to.

monday.com

Reads Items assigned to you or updated by you, boards you own, status changes.

Never reads Boards you aren't on and other people's items beyond status counts.

Docs & notes

Notion

Reads Pages you edited or created in the window: titles, counts and timing.

Never reads Page contents beyond what is needed to title a theme, and pages you didn't touch.

Google Drive and Docs

Reads Files you created or edited in the window: titles, types and dates.

Never reads File contents of other people's documents, and anything marked confidential.

Confluence

Reads Pages you created or edited and comments you made: titles, counts and timing.

Never reads Pages you didn't touch and restricted spaces.

Calendar & mail

Google Calendar

Reads Your calendar events with your own response status: counts, scheduled hours, back-to-back stretches. Aggregates only.

Never reads Attendee lists, meeting notes, and personal or out-of-office blocks (excluded from every count and never mentioned).

Outlook calendar

Reads Your real meetings: counts, hours, back-to-back stretches and timing. Aggregates only.

Never reads Attendee lists, meeting bodies, and personal or private blocks.

Gmail

Reads Your own sent mail: counts, times of day and subject lines.

Never reads Message bodies, anything you received from others, drafts, attachments or labelled-private mail.

Design

Figma

Reads Files you edited or commented on in the window: names, counts and dates.

Never reads Other people's comments and files you haven't touched.

Loom and recordings

Reads Recordings you created or shared, found through your own messages, calendar and docs: date, who it went to by role, what it was for.

Never reads The recording itself or its transcript, and recordings you didn't make or share.

Code

GitHub

Reads Pull requests you opened, reviewed or merged, and issues you touched.

Never reads Other people's code, private repos you haven't named, and review comments from others beyond counts.

GitLab

Reads Merge requests you opened, reviewed or merged, and issues you touched. With no connector, the skill finds your MR links in your own Slack messages and Linear attachments.

Never reads Other people's code and projects you haven't named.

Local git repo

Reads Your own commits across all branches, unmerged branches, stashes and uncommitted changes. Metadata only.

Never reads File contents, other people's commits, or repos you haven't named (the current folder is the default).

Social & other

Salesforce

Reads Opportunities, tasks and activities you own that changed: counts, stages and timing.

Never reads Customer contact details, notes, and other reps' records.

HubSpot

Reads Deals, tasks and activities you own that changed: counts, stages and timing.

Never reads Contact details, email bodies and other owners' records.

Pasted or exported notes

Reads Whatever you paste or point at: a list of what you did, a notes file, an export from any tool, or anything the tools cannot see such as your Slack unread count and saved items. Treated as data, labelled self-reported.

Never reads Anything you didn't hand over.

The skill can only use connectors you have already added to Claude. If you have none, you can paste notes instead.

Privacy and safety

  • It is about you, and only you. Your own work only. No scores, no rankings, no manager's dashboard. It will not review a colleague.
  • Read-only. It never posts, edits or sends anything in your tools.
  • You choose what it reads. Unselected sources are never queried.
  • Private by default. The full report is yours. Colleagues appear by role, never by name. Sensitive topics such as hiring, pay, performance, health and legal matters are kept out of anything shareable.
  • Sharing is a separate step. A share-safe version is built from an allowlist and checked for leaks. You read it, tick a box, then copy it or open LinkedIn with it prefilled. Nothing is posted for you.
  • This website collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies. The page loads two fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google sees your IP address when you visit.

Where your data goes: the skill runs in your own Claude environment and uses the connectors you authorise. Claude and those providers process that data under their own terms, so this is not a promise that nothing leaves your computer. The skill itself uploads nothing and has no server. Get Roasted

Pick the way you use Claude. The skill is free, MIT licensed, and the same in all three.

Claude.ai or Cowork

  1. Download the skill (zip)
  2. Open Claude, go to Settings, then Features (the wording may vary) and upload the zip as a skill.
  3. Make sure code execution is on. Custom skills need a paid plan.
  4. Connect your tools under Connectors, then say Run my Sunday Roast.

Claude Code, plugin

/plugin marketplace add andpurnell-code/sunday-roast
/plugin install sunday-roast@sunday-roast

Then run /sunday-roast:sunday-roast.

Claude Code, plain skill

git clone https://github.com/andpurnell-code/sunday-roast.git
cp -R sunday-roast/plugins/sunday-roast/skills/sunday-roast ~/.claude/skills/

Then run /sunday-roast.

There is no one-click install. The skill needs Python 3.9 or later for its checks. Full steps and troubleshooting are in the install guide. PDF export and hosted share links are not available inside the Claude viewer.
